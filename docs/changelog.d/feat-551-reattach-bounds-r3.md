- A reattach waits at most the mutation timeout plus a 30 s margin (5 min 30 s by default, on the reconciler's clock) for a user cancel of the same run to settle, so one cancel that never ends no longer holds back every run's outcome until the app shuts down; and the action a reattach reports is derived from the phase the row ended as (a run cancelled during the Failed write is reported as the cancel, not as FailedUnrecoverable); a cancel that was cut short (the wait timed out, the app began to shut down, the cancel failed, or its end was not recorded) leaves the row non-terminal and is reported as `CancelInterrupted`, never as a finished cancel or a resumed run, and a shutdown during that wait is reported rather than thrown, so the next launch finishes the cancel (#551, DECISION #599).
