- Prices now come from the Cloud Billing Catalog (issue #214): a read-only gateway (`IPricingCatalogGateway`, real and `FakeGcp`), a pure SKU resolver for g2/a2 machines, Spot and pd-balanced, and `CatalogPricingSource` with a once-a-day cache and the shipped `pricing.json` as the named fallback (`IPricingStatus`: origin, as-of date, reason). `scripts/gen_pricing_json.py` regenerates `pricing.json` from a catalog dump. GCS and egress SKUs and the on-page "prices as of" line are not in this change. The resolver matches the real SKU descriptions (MEASURED 2026-10-16), keeps the shipped Spot price when the catalog gives none, reads the pricing entry in force, does not back off while signed out, ignores a cache made beside an older shipped list, and reports yesterday's prices as stale. The estimate messages no longer say the price list always comes with the app.
