- Prices now come from the Cloud Billing Catalog (issue #214): a read-only gateway (`IPricingCatalogGateway`, real and `FakeGcp`), a pure SKU resolver for g2/a2 machines, Spot and pd-balanced, and `CatalogPricingSource` with a once-a-day cache and the shipped `pricing.json` as the named fallback (`IPricingStatus`: origin, as-of date, reason). `scripts/gen_pricing_json.py` regenerates `pricing.json` from a catalog dump.
