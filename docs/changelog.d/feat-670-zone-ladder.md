- Cloud (#670): the zone ladder is real: zones come from the live accelerator offer (narrowed by quota, bucket continent first), attempts run 3 sequential then 3, 5 and 7 in parallel, the first VM to land is kept at once and the rest are cancelled and deleted by zone (a VM that boots in another zone anyway deletes itself without writing into the job), a zone the user chose is never walked away from, a resume that finds several VMs for one job keeps one and deletes the rest, and zero quota everywhere is reported as the fixable quota error unless the zones really answered "no capacity".
- Cloud (#670 review): the zone claim is written the moment a parallel winner exists (before any extra is deleted) and by every provision, single-zone and reuse included, so a stale claim cannot make a legitimate VM delete itself; a VM in a foreign zone now stops its relay, checks before it writes anything, and shuts the guest down if its self-delete fails.
