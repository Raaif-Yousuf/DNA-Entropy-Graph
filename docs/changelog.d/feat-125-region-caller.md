- feat(worker): every run now also writes `<name>.regions.bed` and `<name>.regions.gff3`, the stretches of low entropy (default below 0.5 bits) and high entropy (default above 1.5 bits) that are at least 20 bases long after merging runs at most 5 bases apart, as a feature track for IGV and other viewers. On a circular molecule a stretch across the origin is one region written as two pieces. New options `analysis.regionThreshold`, `regionMinLength`, `regionMergeGap` (manifest) and `--region-threshold/--region-min-length/--region-merge-gap/--no-regions` (CLI), new `regions` output name; showing the track in the embedded viewer is tracked in #552 (#125) The region options are type-checked on the raw manifest JSON (a float, bool or string is refused, not truncated), and the GFF3 feature type is the Sequence Ontology term `region`.
